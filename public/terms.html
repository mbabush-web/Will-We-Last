What this site is

Will We Last is a free calculator that estimates the odds a relationship will last, based on what happened to couples followed by US government and university surveys. It is for general information and curiosity only.

The numbers describe groups of couples who answered like you. They are not a prediction about your relationship, and they are not medical, legal, financial or relationship advice. Please don't make important decisions based on them. If you are worried about your relationship or your safety, talk to a qualified professional.

Your privacy

  • Your answers stay on your device. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us or stored anywhere.
  • Sharing is up to you. The share button only passes on the result and a link to the site, never your answers, and only when you choose to send it.
  • Visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Emails. If you write to hello@willwelast.app, we see your email address and message, use them only to reply or improve the site, and never sell or share them.
  • Donations. Ko-fi handles donations under its own terms and privacy policy. We only see what Ko-fi shows creators, such as your name and any message you leave.
